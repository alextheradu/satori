# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primarily the author: a high-school student self-studying for the Digital SAT on a laptop, in short daily practice sessions plus occasional full-length timed tests. Open to other students who want to use it, so it must work for someone arriving cold (no tutor, no onboarding call). Context: evenings and weekends, often tired, needing a reason to come back tomorrow.

## Product Purpose

Satori is a Digital SAT practice tool: untimed skill drills with instant explanations, adaptive timed tests, score estimates, and review of missed questions. Success is consistent daily practice that measurably raises the score. The timed test runner deliberately mirrors the official Bluebook app 1:1 so test day feels familiar; everything else is Satori's own.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, encouraging. A quiet study room with a coach who notices your progress. Motivation matters: Duolingo-style momentum (streaks, daily goals, visible progress, small earned celebrations) is welcome, as long as it rewards real work and never becomes noise during a question.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: hero-metric cards, gradient text, identical icon-card grids.
- Khan Academy-style clutter: dense, busy pages where everything competes for attention.
- Gamification that interrupts focus: no celebration mid-question, no mascots shouting during a timed test.

## Design Principles

1. **Focus first, reward after.** While a question is on screen, nothing competes with it. Motivation lives on the boundaries: after an answer, after a session, on Home.
2. **Practice like test day.** The test runner matches Bluebook exactly; never "improve" it away from the real thing.
3. **Progress you can feel.** Every session should leave a visible trace (streak, accuracy, skill movement) so coming back tomorrow feels worth it.
4. **Honest numbers.** Score estimates are labeled as estimates; no inflated praise.
5. **Fast and forgiving.** Instant feedback, keyboard-friendly, easy to undo, never lose progress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, visible focus, full keyboard navigation (practice already supports A–D and Enter). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (celebrations and transitions fall back to fades or nothing). Don't rely on color alone for correct/incorrect.
